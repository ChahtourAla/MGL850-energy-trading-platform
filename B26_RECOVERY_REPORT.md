# B26 recovery report

A missing source waits with backoff. Permanent invalid input and nonpayable drafts do not retry indefinitely; an authorized recovery request may re-enable a run. An uncertain B25 attempt is always recovered by its recorded identity, never blindly rebroadcast. Separate-process tests killed a worker before matching, after committed handoff and after a mined local broadcast. Recovery without the original ephemeral keys returned unresolved for the unbroadcast handoff and verified the original payment after the broadcast.

A local reorganization preserved prior B25 evidence and caused a fresh B26 report to count zero verified paid. Local RPC and the receipt verifier are trusted. This is not Hedera finality. Recovery of new, unsigned allocations after loss of the in-memory signer registry has not been demonstrated and requires a separately reviewed custody or signer-supervision mechanism.
